Compulsory Plantation Activities

Prunning
includes the removal of the dead coffee plant part for better air circulation and sunlight exposure.It keeps the plant healthy and safe
Harvesting
Thecherries are stripped off by machine regardless of whether they are ripe or not.Tractors drive through the rows using vibrators to shake the cherries off the branches.Efficient for commercial production
Drying
After the pulp is removed,coffee is placed in large-rotating metal drums that produce warm air.Suitable since coffee dries easier because the producers have direct control over temperature and air flow
